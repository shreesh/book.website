Catalogue · Sanatan Dharma

Why? Hinduism Defined Scientifically and Rationally

Shastrarth Maharathi Pt. Madhavacharya Shastri

The English edition of the house’s long enquiry into Sanatan Dharma. Each chapter asks why a custom, a samskara, or a rule of daily life exists, and answers from scripture together with a scientific reading — from the morning routine, through the samskaras, to questions of time, direction, and etiquette.
