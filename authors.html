The scholars

Authors

Two names carry the list. Where an edition prints Madhava Shastri or Madhvacharya Shastri, it is the same scholar.

Shastrarth Maharathi

Pt. Madhavacharya Shastri

पं. माधवाचार्य शास्त्री

Scholar of the Vedas, the Puranas, and the practical life of Sanatan Dharma. Editions from this house also print his name as Pt. Madhava Shastri and Pandit Madhvacharya Shastri. The covers style him Shastrarth Maharathi. His books include both the Hindi and the English Why?, Veda Digdarshan, Purana Digdarshan, Our Feasts and Festivals, and The Secrets of Different Ramayanas.

Author

Pt. Premacharya Shastri

पं. प्रेमाचार्य शास्त्री

Writes for readers who want the tradition in plain Hindi: a question-and-answer book on Sanatan Dharma, stories from the Vedas, and a study of Tulsidas. Who is Hindu is the short Sanskrit-and-English introduction listed under his name. With Pt. Madhavacharya Shastri he wrote Different Ramayanas.

  • विविध रामायण — Different Ramayanas
  • Who is Hindu
  • सनातन हिन्दू धर्म प्रश्नोत्तरी — Question Answers on Sanatan Dharma
  • वेदों की श्रेष्ठ कहानियां — Best Stories from the Vedas
  • हिन्दू समाज के उन्नायक: तुलसी दास — Tulsidas: The Nayak of Hindu Society
