Madhav Vidya Bhavan is a Delhi publishing house. Public listings place it in Old Gupta Colony, Vijay Nagar, and date the business to 1960. The books are the work of Pt. Madhavacharya Shastri and Pt. Premacharya Shastri.

The list is concentrated. There are introductions to the Vedas and the Puranas, studies of the different Ramayanas, a book of festivals, stories from the Vedas, and the long work Why?, which asks for a reason behind each practice of Sanatan Dharma. Editions appear in Hindi, in English, and in Sanskrit with Hindi or English beside it.

The catalogue on this site follows the thirteen editions currently offered under the house’s name. A bookseller sometimes prints the imprint as Madhav Vidya Bhawan. The name used here is Madhav Vidya Bhavan.

This site does not take payment. For a copy, write to the house in Vijay Nagar, or use the enquiry page to prepare a note you can send.
